Compliance & Privacy

User Data Deletion Instructions

Platform: SSA Sales Hub & SSA CRM | Last Updated: November 2, 2026

At SSA Sales Hub ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and Meta (Facebook) Platform terms.

If you engaged with our Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Lead Ads, WhatsApp outreach, or accessed our CRM portal, you have the right to request permanent deletion of all your personal records.

How to Request Data Deletion

You can delete your data using either of the two standard methods below:

Method 1: Direct Deletion Request via Email (Fastest)

Send a data deletion request directly to our privacy and data protection team:

  • Email Address: support@ssasaleshub.cloud
  • Subject Line: User Data Deletion Request - [Your Phone / Name]
  • Information to Include:
    • Your full name
    • Your registered phone number (with country code)
    • Your email address associated with the inquiry

Processing Timeline: Our compliance team verifies the request and permanently purges your dossier, contact details, consultation logs, and interaction records from our production databases within 48 to 72 business hours. You will receive an email confirmation with a Deletion Confirmation Reference ID.

Method 2: Remove SSA CRM Permissions from Your Facebook Account

If you logged in or interacted via Facebook / Meta, you can unlink and revoke access directly from your Facebook account:

  1. Log into your Facebook account.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. On the left navigation menu, click Apps and Websites.
  4. Find SSA CRM or SSA Sales Hub in the list of connected applications.
  5. Click Remove.
  6. (Optional) Check the box to "Delete posts, videos or events SSA CRM posted on your timeline" and confirm by clicking Remove.

Facebook will automatically notify our system to invalidate your authentication session and revoke future data sync.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving and confirming your request, we permanently erase:

  • Personal identification data (Full Name, Age, Educational Background)
  • Contact details (Phone Number, Email Address, City/Location)
  • CRM consultation logs, counselor remarks, and status tracking
  • Device tokens (FCM Push Notification tokens)
  • Attribution identifiers (Meta Campaign ID, AdSet ID, Ad ID)
