Privacy Policy for SSA CRM

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the SSA CRM mobile application and platform operated by SSA Sales Hub ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring transparency regarding how information is collected, used, and safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the SSA CRM application, we collect information necessary to provide CRM, lead lifecycle, and communication features:

  • User Account Information: Name, professional email address, phone number, role, and authentication credentials when accessing authorized staff portals.
  • Candidate & Lead Data: Information regarding client inquiries, visa applications, contact information, education, and consultation notes entered into the CRM by authorized staff.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, and unique Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens used strictly for sending operational push alerts.
  • App Permissions:
    • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver timely calendar reminders, lead updates, and team alerts.
    • Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to synchronize CRM records securely with the central database.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for business and CRM operational purposes, including:

  • Authenticating users and providing role-based CRM access.
  • Managing candidate inquiries, visa workflows, and calendar event schedules.
  • Delivering real-time push notifications regarding scheduled callbacks, new assignments, and reminder alerts.
  • Monitoring platform health and debugging unexpected errors.

3. Push Notifications & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

SSA CRM uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to provide push notifications. When you grant notification permission, an anonymous device token is generated by Google Services and transmitted to our server to route notifications directly to your device. You can disable push notifications at any time via your device system settings.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may be processed by trusted infrastructure service providers strictly for delivering app services:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: For reliable notification delivery and push token routing.
  • Cloudflare: For secure hosting and content delivery.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and secure database storage) to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal and CRM data for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal requirements. Users and authorized administrators may request review, modification, or deletion of their account and associated data by contacting our support team.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is an enterprise business software tool intended for professionals and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
